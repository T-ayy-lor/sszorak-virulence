Caustic Globule soaking

Rouse the Brood kicks

Each Rouse the Brood starts every quadrant's kick order over: the first Visceral Burst is player 1's turn, the next is player 2's, and so on. Anyone dead when a cast starts is skipped, so their turn passes to the next player instead of counting against anyone. Correct: kicked on their turn. Missed: their turn, but someone else kicked it or it went off. Out of turn: kicked a cast that was someone else's. A cast that stopped some other way (the Broodling died) still uses up that turn but isn't counted either way. Backup kicks on someone else's turn aren't out of turn, but that turn still counts as their miss; if everyone in a quadrant is dead, the turn goes to the backup. Left and right are as the raid sees it, facing the bosses; front melee is between the two bosses.

Ravenous Feast soaks

Slam 1 is group 1's, slam 2 group 2's, slam 3 group 3's. Correct: soaked your group's slam and the Tainted Blood in the same Ravenous Feast. Wrong group: soaked another group's slam. Missed: didn't soak your group's slam, or soaked it but not the Tainted Blood. Dead players aren't counted.

How this is measured

The same uploader that feeds the Sszorak page reads the combat log for any boss pull with Vexhul or Ithraz in it. Eternal Venom: each new stack is matched to what hit that player in the second around it; Caustic Globule, Venomous Emergence and Corrosive Spit are fine, anything else is unnecessary. Globules: each one belongs to the Caustic Deluge cast before it spawned; a soak is being hit by its rupture. Coiling Ichor: where the player stood when the debuff came off. Quadrants: from where each Broodling was when it cast, relative to the bosses and the middle of the raid. Spell IDs come from the Encounter Journal and the map isn't calibrated yet, so expect adjustments after the first real pulls. Healing includes absorbs and leaves out overhealing. Early deaths are the first 3 deaths of each pull. Kick order and soak groups come from fangs-setup.txt in the repo.
