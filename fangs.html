How this is measured

The uploader script that feeds the Sszorak page also records every cast of spell 1308385 from the combat log: when the cast started, and whether it was interrupted (and by whom), went off, or stopped some other way. It does this in whatever encounter the spell shows up in, so it works from the first Twin Fangs pull without knowing the encounter's ID. A kick's time is how long after that same caster started the cast the interrupt landed. Anything over the late mark (2.0 s by default, matching the Warcraft Logs query) counts as late. Data arrives the same way as the Sszorak page: after each pull, at most one upload every 6 minutes, and GitHub takes a minute or two to publish it. This page checks for new data every 30 seconds. Kick assignments come from fangs-order.json in the repo; the Edit button saves changes in your own browser until a new fangs-order.json is pushed.
